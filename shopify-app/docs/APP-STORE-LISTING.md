## Full description

**Stop wrapping in the back room.** Wrrapd adds a gift-wrap option to your Shopify cart and checkout. When a shopper chooses Wrrapd, we handle the wrapping, the note, optional flowers, and delivery to the gift recipient. Your team keeps fulfilling product — we take the gift presentation and last mile.

**How it works for shoppers**

1. They shop your store as usual.
2. At cart or checkout they choose gift wrap with Wrrapd.
3. They pick wrapping, add a gift message, and optionally add flowers or a custom design.
4. Your order ships to the Wrrapd hub.
5. Wrrapd gift-wraps the order and delivers it to the giftee.

**How it works for merchants**

- Install the free app from the Shopify App Store.
- Turn on Wrrapd gift wrap in the app settings.
- Orders that opt into Wrrapd include clear hub ship-to details.
- Wrrapd notifies your team through the order notes and our ops console.

**Why stores choose Wrrapd**

- You do not need staff time or supplies to wrap gifts.
- Shoppers get a premium gift experience, not a bare box.
- Built for small and growing Shopify stores that want gift-ready checkout without building a wrap desk.
- Backed by Wrrapd Inc. — the same gift-wrap network shoppers already use with our Chrome extension on major retailers.
