Privacy Policy

How Wrrapd collects and uses information from our website, Chrome extension, checkout, and gift-wrapping service.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Wrrapd Inc. (“Wrrapd,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our Chrome extension on supported retailer websites, our website at wrrapd.com, our checkout experience at pay.wrrapd.com, our APIs at api.wrrapd.com, and related fulfillment services (collectively, the “Service”). Please read it together with the Wrrapd Inc. Terms & Conditions shown in the extension at checkout.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in the categories below. We collect only what we reasonably need to operate the gift-wrapping and delivery service, process payments, communicate with you, and meet legal obligations.

1a. From the Chrome extension (on supported retailers)

Our extension works on the checkout pages of the retailers we support: Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, Etsy, Kohl’s, LEGO, Nordstrom, Sephora, and Ulta Beauty. When you use it there, it may read or derive information that is visible in that retailer’s cart, checkout, and gift-options pages in your browser, for example:

  • Product identifiers and titles (for example an Amazon ASIN or a retailer item number) and images for items you select for Wrrapd gift-wrapping or add-ons
  • Gift options you choose (including wrapping style, flowers, messages, and similar selections)
  • Delivery and recipient information shown in the retailer’s checkout (for example recipient or ship-to name, street address, city, state or province, postal code, and country) so we can route wrapped gifts for fulfillment
  • Estimated delivery or arrival information the retailer displays, and the retailer’s order number after you place your order, where we use it to coordinate timing with our operations
  • Technical data such as browser type, extension version, and limited usage or error signals so we can keep the extension reliable

We do not use the extension to collect passwords you type into any retailer site, and we do not collect your payment card details from retailer pages. The extension does not collect unrelated browsing; it only acts on the supported retailers’ cart and checkout pages you use with Wrrapd.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use: Wrrapd’s use of information received from the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use it only to provide the gift-wrapping and delivery service you request; we do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.

1b. Account, checkout, and payment-related information

  • Website account: When you register on wrrapd.com, we collect the information you submit (such as name and email) and credentials managed by our authentication providers.
  • Pay Wrrapd (Helcim): Wrrapd service fees are paid through our checkout hosted on pay.wrrapd.com, which uses our payment processor Helcim (earlier orders used Stripe). Card numbers and card security codes are sent directly to the payment processor; we do not store full payment card numbers on our servers. We receive and retain the processor’s transaction reference, payment status, amount, and limited billing details needed for receipts, refunds, disputes, and accounting.
  • Contact details at checkout: Email and phone number you provide (or confirm) during Pay Wrrapd so we can send confirmations, delivery updates, and support.
  • Text messages and calls: If you text or call our Wrrapd number, we keep the messages, call details, and any photos you send so we can help you. Some simple questions may be answered by an automated assistant, and a team member handles everything else. Reply STOP to stop texts and HELP for help; message and data rates may apply.
  • Billing and shipping details you provide or confirm as part of checkout (for example billing name and address, and shipping or recipient details needed for fulfillment).
  • Gift messages and personalization you enter for recipients.
  • Custom or AI-assisted designs: If you choose custom paper, uploaded art, or AI-assisted designs, related images or files may be transmitted to our systems or cloud storage to produce your order.
  • Optional AI features: If you choose to use optional AI features that rely on a provider API key you supply in your browser, that key is stored locally in your Chrome profile for your device and is not sent to Wrrapd servers unless a feature you invoke explicitly transmits prompts or images to an AI provider you selected.

1c. Automatically collected (website and infrastructure)

  • Cookies and similar technologies on wrrapd.com (for example session, preferences, security, or analytics cookies as configured).
  • Server and application logs (for example IP address, timestamps, and request metadata) for security, debugging, and compliance.
  • Analytics about how the website and extension are used, where enabled.

Location data: We do not collect precise GPS coordinates from the extension or from shoppers. Addresses you provide or confirm for delivery (including through a retailer’s checkout) are used as delivery addresses, not as location tracking.

Proof of delivery: When we deliver a gift, our team takes a photo of the delivered gift and records the time and the delivery location. You can see the photo on your tracking page, and we keep these records to resolve questions about delivery.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, schedule, and complete gift-wrapping, optional flowers, and delivery coordination
  • Process payments, prevent fraud, issue refunds when applicable, and maintain financial records
  • Authenticate accounts, respond to support requests, and send service-related notices
  • Operate video or operational records described in our Terms (for example proof of receipt, wrapping, and handoff where applicable)
  • Improve reliability and safety of the extension, website, and checkout
  • Comply with law, enforce our agreements, and protect rights and safety

3. How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Payment processors (Helcim, and Stripe for earlier orders) to authorize and settle Wrrapd service fees
  • Messaging providers (for example Twilio for text messages and email delivery services) to send order and delivery updates and answer support requests
  • Shipping and operational partners (for example carriers and vendors) as needed to receive goods from retailers, wrap, and deliver
  • Wrrapd gift-wrappers and delivery team members, who see the recipient name, delivery address, gift details, and delivery notes they need to complete your order
  • Cloud hosting, storage, email, analytics, and security vendors under contracts that require appropriate protection of personal data
  • Professional advisors (for example lawyers or accountants) where bound by confidentiality
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by applicable law or legal process
  • Successors in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy or equivalent protections

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing except where you have opted in where required.

4. Financial data and payment processors

Wrrapd service fees are card-not-present payments processed by Helcim (and Stripe for earlier orders). Each processor’s handling of card data is governed by its own policies and PCI requirements. Wrrapd retains payment records needed for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, and customer support (for example transaction references, amounts, timestamps, and customer contact information you provided at checkout).

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods can vary by data type and jurisdiction. You may request deletion of your account where applicable law requires us to honor such requests, subject to exceptions (for example completed payment records we must retain).

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, or to object to certain uses. To exercise rights or ask questions, contact us using the details below. You can also:

  • Update account information in your wrrapd.com account where available
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those emails
  • Remove or disable the Chrome extension; locally stored extension data (such as checkout staging keys or an optional API key you added) can be cleared from Chrome via extension or site data settings

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

We are based in the United States. If you use the Service from other countries, your information may be processed in the United States and other locations where we or our vendors operate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Where changes are material, we will provide additional notice as required by law.
