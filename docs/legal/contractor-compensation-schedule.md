# Wrrapd Compensation Schedule (contractor-facing legal summary)

**Status:** Production draft. Shown in Command Center and incorporated by the WrapStar
Technology Services Agreement §8 and the JoyRider Independent Contractor Agreement §8.
**Do not paste this file onto `wrrapd.com` or the public apply landings.**

Effective: 2026-09-13 until superseded in Command Center → Finance → Hourly rates.

---

## 1. Form of pay

Wrrapd pays WrapStars, JoyRiders, and WrapRiders an **hourly rate**. Wrrapd does **not** pay a
per-order piece rate. Dollar amounts are published in Command Center and, once
activated, in the contractor app. Amounts may differ by **ZIP code** (exact ZIP or
3-digit prefix) and by role. Each role — WrapStar, JoyRider, WrapRider — has its own
rate schedule.

## 2. ZIP lookup

For the contractor’s home / assigned work ZIP: exact ZIP override, else 3-digit
prefix override, else the role default.

## 3. WrapStar pace

The expected wrapping pace is **twelve (12) finished gifts per paid hour**.

If fewer than 12 gifts are finished in a paid hour, compensation for that hour is
reduced by:

**shortfall × (that hour’s hourly rate ÷ 12)**

where shortfall = 12 − finished gifts (not less than zero).

A gift is “finished” when it is wrapped to published standards, documented, labeled
(including the delivery barcode), and ready for courier pickup.

## 4. WrapStar materials

WrapStars furnish, at their own expense: gift-wrapping paper, small boxes when
needed, scissors, cutters, and tape, plus ordinary ribbon/tissue unless an order
states that Wrrapd will supply a material.

## 5. JoyRider work (hourly, no 12-gift pace)

A JoyRider’s hourly work includes: pickup of inbound items from a nearby Wrrapd
location (for example a PO Box); drop to assigned WrapStar(s); pickup of flowers
and finished wraps; scan of each wrap barcode for address and flower pairing; and
final delivery of gifts and/or flowers.

## 5a. WrapRider work (both clocks)

A WrapRider is engaged to wrap **and** deliver under a single **WrapRider hourly
rate** (published in Command Center → Finance → Hourly rates, by ZIP). The
12-gifts/hour pace applies to wrapping hours only; there is no pace on delivery
hours. Materials rules in §4 apply to wrapping hours; vehicle/insurance rules in
the WrapRider agreement apply to delivery hours.

## 6. Accrual and pay calendar

Hours accrue for accepted windows actually worked, prorated to 15 minutes.
Wrrapd pays on the then-published payout calendar (direct deposit). Wrrapd may
offset or withhold for fraud, failed proof, overpayment, or amounts the contractor
owes Wrrapd.

## 7. Independent contractor

Contractors are not employees. Wrrapd does not withhold income or payroll taxes
except as required by law and may issue Form 1099-NEC.

## 8. Changes

Wrrapd may update this Schedule prospectively by changing Command Center rates or
publishing a revised Schedule in the contractor app. Material methodology changes
(for example, returning to per-order pay) require notice via the app or email and
apply only going forward.
