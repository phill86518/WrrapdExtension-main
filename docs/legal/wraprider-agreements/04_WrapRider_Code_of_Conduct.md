# WRAPRIDER CODE OF CONDUCT AND PLATFORM GUIDELINES

**Wrrapd, Inc.** — Wrap + Deliver  
**Companion document** to the WrapRider Independent Contractor Technology Services Agreement

**Status:** Production draft for licensed counsel review before BoldSign upload.

---

Contractor agrees to follow these standards while providing wrapping and delivery Services. Wrrapd may update published standards in the Apps; material safety rules apply as soon as posted.

## 1. Craft

- Wrap to published Wrrapd presentation standards. Every order is someone’s occasion.
- Use a clean, dedicated wrapping area. Keep customer merchandise off the floor and away from pets, food, and smoke.
- Document each wrap as the WrapStar App requires (video while in your care; photos of the finished gift and barcode).
- Affix the delivery barcode so it scans cleanly. Do not cover it with extra ribbon in a way that hides the code.

## 2. Road and delivery

- Hold a valid license, eligible vehicle, and legally required auto insurance whenever you accept delivery work.
- Do not drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or anything that impairs safe driving.
- Follow traffic laws. Do not use a handheld phone while the vehicle is in motion.
- Scan barcodes before final delivery. Deliver to the address the App shows. Capture delivery proof.
- Do not enter a home unless the App and the recipient clearly invite a handoff at the threshold; leave packages as the App instructs when no one is home.

## 3. People and privacy

- Be courteous at the door. Do not discuss other customers’ orders.
- Do not contact giftees except as the JoyRider App requires for a delivery attempt.
- Do not post customer names, addresses, or gift contents on social media.

## 4. Honesty

- Do not accept work you cannot complete in the agreed window.
- Report damage, shortage, or suspected tampering immediately.
- Do not open finished wraps. Do not keep customer merchandise.

## 5. Platform

- Keep both Apps reasonably up to date.
- Do not share login credentials.
- Follow deactivation and return-of-goods instructions if Wrrapd suspends access.

Violation of this Code may result in lost offers, suspension, or termination of the Agreement, consistent with the Services Agreement.
