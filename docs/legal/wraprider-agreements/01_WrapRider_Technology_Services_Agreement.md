This WrapRider Independent Contractor Technology Services Agreement (the “**Agreement**”) is entered into by and between **Wrrapd, Inc.**, a company doing business as Wrrapd (“**Wrrapd**” or the “**Company**”), and the undersigned individual or business entity (“**Contractor**” or “**WrapRider**”). Wrrapd and Contractor are each a “**Party**” and together the “**Parties**.”

By signing, Contractor acknowledges that Contractor has read, understands, and agrees to be bound by this Agreement and by the concurrently executed Mutual Arbitration Agreement, Background Check and Motor Vehicle Record Authorization, WrapRider Code of Conduct, and Third-Party Litigation Funding Disclosure (collectively, the “**Companion Documents**”), and by the then-current **Compensation Schedule**.

## 1. Purpose and Relationship

1.1 Wrrapd operates a technology platform that arranges professional gift wrapping and delivery. Contractor provides **both** (a) gift-wrapping of merchandise at an approved wrapping location and (b) courier and logistics Services, including inbound package collection, transfers, floral pickup when assigned, barcode scanning, and final-mile delivery (together, the “**Services**”), as an independent contractor.

1.2 Contractor will use the dedicated **WrapStar mobile application** (the “**WrapStar App**”) for wrapping work and the dedicated **JoyRider mobile application** (the “**JoyRider App**”) for logistics and delivery, together with related web or portal tools (collectively, the “**Platform**”). Each App is contractor-facing and separate from any consumer-facing Wrrapd application.

1.3 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or general agency relationship. Contractor is not authorized to bind Wrrapd or to represent that Contractor is an employee of Wrrapd.

---

## 2. Independent Contractor Status

2.1 Contractor is and shall remain an **independent contractor**. Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes, withholdings, and filings arising from compensation under this Agreement. Wrrapd will issue IRS Form **1099-NEC** (or successor) as required by law.

2.2 Contractor shall provide, at Contractor’s own expense except as Wrrapd expressly agrees in writing:

(a) For wrapping: the labor, an approved wrapping location, a compatible smartphone capable of running the WrapStar App, camera capability for required proof, internet access, and **ordinary wrapping materials**, including gift-wrapping paper, small boxes when an item requires a box, scissors, cutters, and tape;

(b) For delivery: a compatible smartphone capable of running the JoyRider App, internet access, fuel, and an eligible, legally operated vehicle with **insurance required by law to drive**.

Wrrapd does **not** require Contractor to purchase commercial general liability or cargo insurance beyond legally required automobile insurance, unless a future written amendment states otherwise.

2.3 Subject to Platform rules and brand, quality, proof, safety, scan, and timing standards published by Wrrapd, Contractor controls the manner and means of performing the Services.

2.4 Contractor may accept or decline wrap orders and delivery windows as the Apps permit, may provide similar services to others, and is not guaranteed any minimum volume, hours, or income.

2.5 Contractor shall not subcontract, assign, or delegate performance of the Services to any third party without Wrrapd’s prior written consent.

3.1 Contractor represents and warrants that: (a) if an individual, Contractor is at least **twenty-one (21)** years of age; (b) Contractor holds a valid U.S. driver’s license and is legally authorized to drive and to work in the United States in the areas where Contractor operates; (c) Contractor will maintain vehicle insurance required by applicable law; (d) if an entity, the person signing has full authority to bind the entity; (e) all information provided in Contractor’s application, W-9, and onboarding materials is true and complete; and (f) Contractor is not subject to any agreement that would conflict with this Agreement.

3.2 Contractor shall promptly update Wrrapd if any representation in this Section 3 becomes inaccurate (including license suspension or insurance lapse).

## 4. Scope of Services (Wrap and Deliver)

4.1 Contractor’s role is **gift wrapping and delivery**. Unlike a WrapStar-only contractor, Contractor **does** operate a vehicle and **does** meet gift recipients at the door when delivering. Unlike a JoyRider-only contractor, Contractor **does** wrap gifts.

4.2 **Wrapping.** Contractor agrees to:

(a) Maintain an approved **wrapping location** and agreed **handoff windows** so inbound packages and finished wraps can move on schedule. When Contractor is also performing the courier hop, Contractor may drop inbound packages to Contractor’s own wrapping location and later collect finished wraps from that location;

(b) When beginning work on a package, inspect for visible damage, shortage, or suspected tampering, and report the same to Wrrapd promptly and in any event within the timeframe published in Platform standards (and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery);

(c) Professionally gift-wrap items to Wrrapd brand standards using approved materials, techniques, and any order-specific designs or messages;

(d) Record and upload clear **video and/or other proof** through the WrapStar App as required by Section 6;

(e) Affix the Platform **barcode / delivery label** to each finished gift, include the label in finished-wrap documentation, and stage wrapped orders for delivery according to Platform instructions;

(f) Maintain a clean, professional wrapping workspace; and

(g) Install and use the WrapStar App, keep it reasonably up to date, and follow in-App workflows for availability, handoff windows, status updates, communications, and proof uploads.

4.3 **Logistics and delivery.** As assigned in the JoyRider App, Contractor agrees to:

(a) Collect retailer or Company packages from a nearby Wrrapd location (including a PO Box, hub, or other address Wrrapd designates) when that hop is assigned;

(b) When an order requires flowers, obtain them as the App instructs;

(c) Before final delivery, scan the barcode on each wrapped gift (and any flower-only label). The App will display the delivery address and whether flowers accompany that gift;

(d) Deliver gifts and/or flowers to the giftee and capture any required delivery proof (for example, a photo);

(e) Exercise reasonable care as a bailee from pickup until delivery or return to a Wrrapd location; and

(f) Install and use the JoyRider App, keep it reasonably up to date, and follow in-App workflows for offers, navigation, scans, and delivery proof.

4.4 Contractor shall not contact customers or giftees except as the JoyRider App requires for a delivery attempt (for example, a brief arrival or access message). Contractor shall not open a finished wrap to read a packing slip.

4.5 Wrrapd may update published wrapping, scan, and delivery standards prospectively via the Platform or email.

## 5. Custody of Goods; Care of Merchandise

5.1 From the time Contractor takes possession of customer merchandise, wrapping materials supplied for an order, or finished gifts until Contractor completes delivery or releases the goods to a Company designee in accordance with Platform instructions, Contractor shall exercise **reasonable care** as a bailee for the benefit of Wrrapd and its customers.

5.2 Contractor is responsible for loss, theft, damage, misdelivery, or unauthorized use of goods to the extent caused by Contractor’s **negligence, willful misconduct, unauthorized use, or failure to exercise reasonable care**. Contractor is **not** responsible for damage that existed before possession and that Contractor timely reported, or for loss after confirmed delivery per Platform rules, or for loss caused by Wrrapd’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

---

## 6. Proof; Documentation; Barcode

6.1 Contractor shall capture wrapping proof (unboxing → wrap → finished gift) in the WrapStar App and delivery proof in the JoyRider App as the Platform requires.

6.2 Contractor shall affix and scan barcodes as the Apps instruct. The barcode is how the Platform pairs a finished wrap with a delivery address and any flowers.

---

## 7. Vehicle; License; Insurance

7.1 Contractor shall maintain a valid driver license and an eligible personal vehicle, and shall not drive for Wrrapd work while license, registration, or legally required insurance is lapsed or suspended.

7.2 Contractor shall provide proof of legally required automobile insurance upon request and whenever coverage changes.

## 8. Compensation

8.1 Contractor is paid an **hourly rate** under the then-current Compensation Schedule published in Command Center and shown in the contractor apps. Wrapping hours use the WrapStar rate and expected wrapping pace. Delivery hours use the JoyRider rate. Amounts may differ by ZIP. Wrrapd does **not** pay a per-order piece rate unless a future Schedule expressly says so.

8.2 Hours accrue for accepted windows actually worked, prorated as the Schedule states. Wrrapd may offset or withhold for fraud, failed proof, overpayment, or amounts Contractor owes Wrrapd.

8.3 Contractor is not entitled to employee benefits. Tips, if any, follow the then-current Schedule; unless the Schedule says otherwise, wrapping hours do not include customer tips.

9.1 Contractor shall not disclose customer, giftee, or order information except as needed to perform the Services.
