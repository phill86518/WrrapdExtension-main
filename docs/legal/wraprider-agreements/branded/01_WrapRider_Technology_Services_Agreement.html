WRAPRIDER INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

TECHNOLOGY SERVICES AGREEMENT

Wrrapd, Inc. — Gift Wrapping Network · WrapRider App

Scope note: Under this Agreement, WrapRiders provide gift-wrapping services and logistics / final-mile delivery. Contractor uses the WrapRider App.

This WrapRider Independent Contractor Technology Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into by and between Wrrapd, Inc., a company doing business as Wrrapd, with notice and legal address at 7901 4th Street N, Ste. 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702-4399 (“Wrrapd” or the “Company”), and the undersigned individual or business entity (“Contractor” or “WrapRider”). Wrrapd and Contractor are each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

By signing, Contractor acknowledges that Contractor has read, understands, and agrees to be bound by this Agreement and by the concurrently executed Mutual Arbitration Agreement, Background Check and Motor Vehicle Record Authorization, WrapRider Code of Conduct, and Third-Party Litigation Funding Disclosure (collectively, the “Companion Documents”), and by the then-current Compensation Schedule.

1. Purpose and Relationship

1.1 Wrrapd operates a technology platform that arranges professional gift wrapping and delivery. Contractor provides both (a) gift-wrapping of merchandise at an approved wrapping location and (b) courier and logistics Services, including inbound package collection, transfers, floral pickup when assigned, barcode scanning, and final-mile delivery (together, the “Services”), as an independent contractor.

1.2 Contractor will use the dedicated WrapRider mobile application (the “WrapRider App”) together with related web or portal tools (collectively, the “Platform”). The WrapRider App is contractor-facing and separate from any consumer-facing Wrrapd application.

1.3 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or general agency relationship. Contractor is not authorized to bind Wrrapd or to represent that Contractor is an employee of Wrrapd.

2. Independent Contractor Status

2.1 Contractor is and shall remain an independent contractor. Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes, withholdings, and filings arising from compensation under this Agreement. Wrrapd will issue IRS Form 1099-NEC (or successor) as required by law.

2.2 Contractor shall provide, at Contractor’s own expense except as Wrrapd expressly agrees in writing:

(a) For wrapping: the labor, an approved wrapping location, a compatible smartphone capable of running the WrapRider App, camera capability for required live session and proof, internet access, and ordinary wrapping materials, including gift-wrapping paper, small boxes when an item requires a box, scissors, cutters, and tape;

(b) For delivery: a compatible smartphone capable of running the WrapRider App, internet access, fuel, and an eligible, legally operated vehicle with insurance required by law to drive.

Wrrapd does not require Contractor to purchase commercial general liability or cargo insurance beyond legally required automobile insurance, unless a future written amendment states otherwise.

2.3 Subject to Platform rules and brand, quality, proof, safety, scan, and timing standards published by Wrrapd, Contractor controls the manner and means of performing the Services.

2.4 Contractor may accept or decline wrap orders and delivery windows as the WrapRider App permits, may provide similar services to others, and is not guaranteed any minimum volume, hours, or income.

2.5 Contractor shall not subcontract, assign, or delegate performance of the Services to any third party without Wrrapd’s prior written consent.

3. Eligibility; Capacity; Representations

3.1 Contractor represents and warrants that: (a) if an individual, Contractor is at least twenty-one (21) years of age; (b) Contractor holds a valid U.S. driver’s license and is legally authorized to drive and to work in the United States in the areas where Contractor operates; (c) Contractor will maintain vehicle insurance required by applicable law; (d) if an entity, the person signing has full authority to bind the entity; (e) all information provided in Contractor’s application, W-9, license, driving-abstract, and onboarding materials is true and complete; and (f) Contractor is not subject to any agreement that would conflict with this Agreement.

3.2 Contractor shall promptly update Wrrapd if any representation in this Section 3 becomes inaccurate (including license suspension or insurance lapse).

4. Scope of Services (Wrap and Deliver)

4.1 Contractor’s role is gift wrapping and delivery. Contractor does operate a vehicle and does meet gift recipients at the door when delivering. Contractor does wrap gifts.

4.2 Wrapping. Contractor agrees to:

(a) Maintain an approved wrapping location and agreed handoff windows so inbound packages and finished wraps can move on schedule. When Contractor is also performing the courier hop, Contractor may drop inbound packages to Contractor’s own wrapping location and later collect finished wraps from that location;

(b) When beginning work on a package, inspect for visible damage, shortage, or suspected tampering, and report the same to Wrrapd promptly and in any event within the timeframe published in Platform standards (and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery);

(c) Professionally gift-wrap items to Wrrapd brand standards using approved materials, techniques, and any order-specific designs or messages;

(d) Start a live session and record and upload clear video and/or other proof through the WrapRider App as required by Section 6;

(e) Affix the Platform barcode / delivery label to each finished gift, include the label in finished-wrap documentation, and stage wrapped orders for delivery according to Platform instructions;

(f) Maintain a clean, professional wrapping workspace; and

(g) Install and use the WrapRider App, keep it reasonably up to date, and follow in-App workflows for availability, handoff windows, status updates, communications, live session, and proof uploads.

4.3 Logistics and delivery. As assigned in the WrapRider App, Contractor agrees to:

(a) Collect retailer or Company packages from a nearby Wrrapd location (including a PO Box, hub, or other address Wrrapd designates) when that hop is assigned;

(b) When an order requires flowers, obtain them as the App instructs;

(c) Before leaving for the giftee, scan the barcode on each expected wrap unit (and any flower-only label). The App will show completeness and, after a valid scan, the delivery address and whether flowers accompany that gift. Contractor shall not mark en-route until every expected code for that stop is scanned;

(d) Deliver gifts and/or flowers to the giftee, scan again at the door as the App requires, and capture any required delivery proof (for example, a photo);

(e) Exercise reasonable care as a bailee from pickup until delivery or return to a Wrrapd location; and

(f) Follow in-App workflows for offers, navigation, scans, and delivery proof.

4.4 Contractor shall not contact customers or giftees except as the WrapRider App requires for a delivery attempt (for example, a brief arrival or access message). Contractor shall not open a finished wrap to read a packing slip.

4.4A Wrrapd dispatch only. Contractor shall perform wrapping and delivery Services only through the WrapRider App. Contractor shall not accept, dispatch, or complete Wrrapd work through DoorDash, Uber, Spark, Shipt, Instacart, or any other third-party gig platform.

4.5 Wrrapd may update published wrapping, scan, and delivery standards prospectively via the Platform or email.

5. Custody of Goods; Care of Merchandise

5.1 From the time Contractor takes possession of customer merchandise, wrapping materials supplied for an order, or finished gifts until Contractor completes delivery or releases the goods to a person Wrrapd assigns in accordance with Platform instructions, Contractor shall exercise reasonable care as a bailee for the benefit of Wrrapd and its customers.

5.2 Contractor is responsible for loss, theft, damage, misdelivery, or unauthorized use of goods to the extent caused by Contractor’s negligence, willful misconduct, unauthorized use, or failure to exercise reasonable care. Contractor is not responsible for damage that existed before possession and that Contractor timely reported, or for loss after confirmed delivery per Platform rules, or for loss caused by Wrrapd’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

5.3 Contractor shall not use, consume, gift, sell, or appropriate customer merchandise or flowers for personal purposes, and shall not leave goods unsecured.

5.4 Title to customer merchandise does not pass to Contractor.

5A. Refusal, Detention, and Demand-for-Return

5A.1 Duty to perform or release. If Contractor will not wrap an accepted order, will not deliver an accepted load, will not complete required scans, or will not return goods, Contractor shall say so in the WrapRider App immediately and shall make every item available for pickup by the person Wrrapd assigns within two (2) hours of that notice (or the shorter window then published in the App).

5A.2 No lien. Contractor has no right to hold, detain, hide, or condition the return of goods for unpaid compensation, pace disputes, ratings, or any other claim against Wrrapd.

5A.3 Demand. Wrrapd may demand return of any goods at any time. Contractor shall stage the goods at the door or wrapping entrance, or at a curb / hub Wrrapd designates. Failure to make the goods available by the stated deadline is conversion and unauthorized detention. Wrrapd may report the matter to law enforcement, withhold or claw back pay, charge replacement cost of goods plus wrap, materials, and makeup delivery, and terminate immediately.

5A.4 Access. Contractor has no right to enter a giftee’s home. Contractor has no right to require Wrrapd personnel or any person Wrrapd assigns to enter Contractor’s home. Goods must be staged at the door, wrapping entrance, or designated curb.

5A.5 This Section 5A is a material term. Breach is grounds for immediate suspension and termination, in addition to any civil or criminal remedies.

6. Live Session, Recording, Proof, and Barcode

6.1 Live wrap session. For every wrapping order, Contractor shall start a live session in the WrapRider App before unboxing and keep the session running through inspection, wrapping, sealing, barcode affix, and the finished-gift photo. Wrrapd may observe the session in real time. A recording is stored. The camera shall stay on the gift and wrapping surface, not on housemates, children, or private areas of the home. If the live link drops, Contractor shall stop wrapping, restart the session, and then continue.

6.2 Contractor shall capture wrapping proof (unboxing → wrap → finished gift, including the affixed barcode) and delivery proof (scans plus photo) in the WrapRider App as the Platform requires.

6.3 Contractor shall affix and scan barcodes as the App instructs. The barcode is how the Platform pairs a finished wrap with a delivery address and any flowers. Contractor shall not mark a wrap ready without every unit labeled, and shall not mark a stop en-route until every expected code is scanned.

6.4 Failure to start or maintain the live wrap session, or failed or fabricated scans or proof, may result in non-payment, clawback, suspension, and/or termination.

6.5 Contractor grants Wrrapd a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use live-session media and proof media for quality assurance, customer support, dispute resolution, claims handling, training, legal compliance, and Platform operation. Live wrap sessions are for Wrrapd staff only and are not a public stream.

7. Vehicle; License; Insurance

7.1 Contractor shall maintain a valid driver license and an eligible personal vehicle, and shall not drive for Wrrapd work while license, registration, or legally required insurance is lapsed or suspended.

7.2 Contractor shall provide proof of legally required automobile insurance upon request and whenever coverage changes. Coverage from DoorDash, Uber, Spark, Shipt, or any other gig platform applies only to that platform’s trips and does not cover Wrrapd Services. Contractor shall not rely on that coverage for a Wrrapd hop.

8. Compensation

8.1 Contractor is paid an hourly rate under the then-current WrapRider Compensation Schedule published in Command Center and shown in the WrapRider App. Amounts may differ by ZIP. Wrrapd does not pay a per-order piece rate unless a future Schedule expressly says so.

8.2 Wrapping hours — minimum and fractional pace. For wrapping work, the conversion rate is twelve (12) finished gifts = one (1) paid hour. Paid wrapping hours for an accepted wrap window equal the greater of (a) one (1) hour, or (b) finished gifts ÷ 12 (including fractional hours). Examples: four (4) finished gifts still earn one (1) full paid hour; thirty-seven (37) finished gifts earn three (3) hours plus one-twelfth (1/12) of an hour. A gift is finished when wrapped to published standards, documented (live session and proof), labeled (including the delivery barcode), and ready for delivery.

8.3 Delivery hours. Delivery / logistics hours accrue for accepted delivery windows actually worked, as recorded in the WrapRider App, with a minimum of one (1) hour for each accepted delivery window actually commenced. There is no twelve-gifts pace on delivery hours.

8.4 Incentive compensation. From time to time — particularly during higher-demand periods — Wrrapd may offer discretionary incentive or bonus compensation, including additional pay for wrapping above the twelve-gifts-per-hour pace, peak-period bonuses, or other incentives published in the Compensation Schedule, Command Center, or WrapRider App. Incentive terms apply only for the periods stated and create no ongoing entitlement afterward.

8.5 Wrrapd may offset or withhold for fraud, failed proof, skipped live session, overpayment, no-show costs under Section 16A, amounts under Section 5A, or other amounts Contractor owes Wrrapd.

8.6 Contractor is not entitled to employee benefits. Tips, if any, follow the then-current Schedule; unless the Schedule says otherwise, wrapping hours do not include customer tips.

9.2 Contractor shall keep WrapRider App credentials confidential and shall not share Contractor’s account.

16A. Weekly Availability; Unavailability; Cancellation; Return of Goods

16A.1 Weekly availability (required). Contractor shall submit Contractor’s availability for the following calendar week (Monday through Sunday) through the WrapRider App no later than Friday at 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time of the week before that work week. Availability is used to allocate wrapping and delivery work. Failure to submit by the deadline may result in Contractor being treated as unavailable for that week (unless Wrrapd overrides in Command Center), with no guaranteed hours.

16A.2 Immediate notice — illness, bereavement, emergency. If Contractor cannot work an accepted wrap window or delivery window, or any portion of a submitted available day, because of sudden illness, bereavement, emergency, or any other unforeseen cause, Contractor shall notify Wrrapd immediately through the WrapRider App (or the then-published emergency contact channel) — and in any event as soon as Contractor knows, and no later than four (4) hours before the published start of the affected window. Silence is a material breach.

16A.3 Return of deliverable materials. Upon any such notice (or upon Wrrapd’s demand), Contractor shall make all customer merchandise, finished or unfinished wraps, packages in transit, labels, barcodes, flowers, and related deliverable materials in Contractor’s possession available for pickup by a backup contractor or other person Wrrapd assigns, staged for handoff within two (2) hours (or the shorter window then published), consistent with Section 5A. Contractor shall not detain goods.

16A.4 Personal liability for delay or detention. If Contractor fails to give immediate notice under Section 16A.2, fails to return or stage goods under Section 16A.3 / Section 5A, or otherwise causes delay, missed customer promises, or reassignment cost: (a) the missed window is unpaid; (b) Wrrapd may withhold, offset, or claw back compensation and recover reasonable last-minute reassignment, replacement, and makeup-delivery costs from amounts otherwise owed to Contractor; (c) Contractor remains personally responsible under Sections 5 and 5A for loss, damage, misdelivery, or detention of goods; and (d) repeated late cancels or no-shows are grounds for immediate termination.

16A.5 Illness, bereavement, or emergency does not excuse the notice or return duties in this Section 16A. Wrrapd’s use of backup contractors is an operations matter and does not create a right to keep goods or to be paid for a missed window.
