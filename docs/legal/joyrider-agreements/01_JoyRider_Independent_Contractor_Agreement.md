# JOYRIDER INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

**Wrrapd, Inc.** — Gift Wrapping Network · JoyRider App

**Status:** Production draft for licensed counsel review before BoldSign upload.  
**Scope:** JoyRiders provide **logistics and final-mile delivery** via the **JoyRider App**. Gift wrapping is a separate WrapStar function and is **not** a JoyRider duty.

---

This JoyRider Independent Contractor Agreement (the “**Agreement**”) is entered into by and between **Wrrapd, Inc.** (“**Wrrapd**”) and the undersigned (“**Contractor**” or “**JoyRider**”).

By signing, Contractor agrees to this Agreement and to the then-current Compensation Schedule and applicable Companion Documents (arbitration, background check, code of conduct, litigation-funding disclosure) presented at onboarding.

---

## 1. Purpose and relationship

1.1 Wrrapd operates a technology platform that arranges professional gift wrapping and delivery. Contractor provides **courier and logistics Services** as an independent contractor using the **JoyRider App** and related tools (the “**Platform**”).

1.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates employment, partnership, or agency. Contractor is not authorized to bind Wrrapd.

---

## 2. Independent contractor status

2.1 Contractor is an **independent contractor**, responsible for Contractor’s own taxes, vehicle, insurance required by law to drive, fuel, and phone. Wrrapd will issue IRS Form **1099-NEC** as required.

2.2 Contractor controls the manner and means of driving and delivery, subject to published safety, proof, scan, and timing standards.

2.3 Contractor is not guaranteed any minimum hours, volume, or income.

---

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 Contractor represents that Contractor is at least **twenty-one (21)** years of age, holds a valid U.S. driver license, is legally authorized to work in the United States, and will maintain required vehicle insurance.

---

## 4. Scope of Services (logistics only)

4.1 Contractor’s role is **pickup, transfer, floral pickup when assigned, barcode scan, and delivery**. Contractor does not wrap gifts.

4.2 Contractor agrees to, as assigned in the JoyRider App:

**(a) Inbound pickup.** Collect retailer or Company packages from a nearby Wrrapd location (including a PO Box, hub, or other address Wrrapd designates);

**(b) WrapStar drop.** Deliver those packages to the assigned WrapStar(s) during the agreed handoff window;

**(c) Flowers and finished wraps.** When an order requires flowers, obtain them as the App instructs; collect finished wrapped gifts from the WrapStar(s);

**(d) Barcode scan.** Before final delivery, scan the barcode on each wrapped gift (and any flower-only label). The App will display the delivery address and whether flowers accompany that gift. Contractor shall not open a finished wrap to read a packing slip;

**(e) Final delivery.** Deliver gifts and/or flowers to the giftee and capture any required delivery proof;

**(f) Care.** Exercise reasonable care as a bailee from pickup until delivery or return to a Wrrapd location.

4.3 Contractor shall not contact customers except as the JoyRider App requires for a delivery attempt.

---

## 5. Compensation

5.1 Contractor is paid an **hourly rate**, not a per-order piece rate. The rate is determined by the **Compensation Schedule** then published in Command Center / the JoyRider App, which may vary by **ZIP code** (exact ZIP or 3-digit prefix) or use the JoyRider default.

5.2 Hours accrue for accepted windows actually worked, as recorded in the JoyRider App, prorated to fifteen (15) minutes.

5.3 Wrrapd does not guarantee hours. Tips, if ever enabled for JoyRiders, follow the then-current Compensation Schedule.

5.4 Wrrapd may withhold, offset, or claw back compensation for fraud, failed or fabricated proof, failed scans used to conceal a miss, chargebacks, negligence, overpayments, or other amounts Contractor owes Wrrapd.

5.5 Contractor shall complete Form W-9 before payouts.

5.6 Material changes to compensation methodology will be communicated via the JoyRider App or email and apply prospectively.

---

## 6. Vehicle, license, and safety

6.1 Contractor shall maintain a valid license, an eligible vehicle, and legally required insurance, and shall follow published safety standards.

6.2 Contractor shall not drive while impaired and shall obey traffic laws.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Addresses, recipient names, order contents, barcode payloads, and Platform information are Confidential Information. Contractor shall not share, store, or reuse them except to complete an assigned stop.

---

## 8. Term and termination

8.1 Either party may end this relationship as permitted by the Companion Documents and applicable law. Upon termination Contractor shall cease use of the JoyRider App and return Wrrapd property (including unused labels).

---

## 9. Entire agreement

9.1 This Agreement, the Companion Documents, and the Compensation Schedule then in effect are the entire agreement regarding the Services. Platform policies may be updated prospectively.
