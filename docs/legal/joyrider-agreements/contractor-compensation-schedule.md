# JoyRider Compensation Schedule

**Do not paste this file onto `wrrapd.com` or the public apply landings.**

Effective: 2026-09-22 until superseded in Command Center → Finance → Hourly rates.

**Notice / legal address:** 7901 4th Street N, Ste. 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702-4399

---

## 1. Form of pay

Wrrapd pays JoyRiders an **hourly rate**. Wrrapd does **not** pay a per-order piece rate.
Dollar amounts are published in Command Center and, once activated, in the JoyRider App.
Amounts may differ by **ZIP code** (exact ZIP or 3-digit prefix).

## 2. ZIP lookup

For the contractor’s home / assigned work ZIP: exact ZIP override, else 3-digit
prefix override, else the JoyRider default.

## 3. Work covered (no wrapping pace)

A JoyRider’s hourly work includes: pickup of inbound items from a nearby Wrrapd
location (for example a PO Box); drop to the wrapping location Wrrapd assigns;
pickup of flowers and finished wraps; completeness scan of each wrap barcode for
address and flower pairing; and final delivery of gifts and/or flowers.

A wrapping-pace rule does **not** apply to JoyRider hours.

## 4. Minimum hour and incentives

Each accepted delivery window actually commenced is paid **not less than one (1) hour**.

From time to time — especially in higher-demand periods — Wrrapd may publish
**discretionary incentive or bonus compensation** (peak-period or completion bonuses).
Terms apply only for the stated period.

## 5. Accrual and pay calendar

Hours accrue under Section 4 for accepted windows actually worked. Wrrapd pays on
the then-published payout calendar (direct deposit). Wrrapd may offset or withhold
for fraud, failed scans or proof, overpayment, no-show reassignment cost, or amounts
the contractor owes Wrrapd.

A late cancel or silent no-show of an accepted window is unpaid.

## 6. Independent contractor

Contractors are not employees. Wrrapd does not withhold income or payroll taxes
except as required by law and may issue Form 1099-NEC.

## 7. Changes

Wrrapd may update this Schedule prospectively by changing Command Center rates or
publishing a revised Schedule in the JoyRider App. Material methodology changes
(for example, returning to per-order pay) require notice via the app or email and
apply only going forward.
