**Wrrapd, Inc.** — Gift Wrapping Network · WrapStar App

**Scope note:** Under this Agreement, WrapStars provide **gift-wrapping services only** via the **WrapStar App**. Packages are brought to the wrapping location and collected when wrapping is done. Final-mile delivery, PO Box collection, floral purchasing, and driving are **not** WrapStar duties.

## 1. Purpose and Relationship

1.1 Wrrapd operates a technology platform and related services that connect customers with independent contractors who provide professional **gift-wrapping** of merchandise and related items (the “**Services**”). Final-mile delivery, courier transport, PO Box collection, floral purchasing, and driving are **outside the scope** of the Services under this Agreement and are performed by persons Wrrapd assigns, not by WrapStars.

1.2 Contractor desires to provide the Services as an independent contractor using Wrrapd’s systems, including the dedicated **WrapStar mobile application** (the “**WrapStar App**”), related web or portal tools, order assignment and status tools, Contractor’s approved **wrapping location**, and related systems (collectively, the “**Platform**”). The WrapStar App is a contractor-facing application that is **separate from** any consumer-facing Wrrapd application.

1.3 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or general agency relationship. Contractor is not authorized to bind Wrrapd or to represent that Contractor is an employee of Wrrapd.

## 4. Scope of Services (Wrapping Only)

4.1 Contractor’s role is **gift wrapping only**. Contractor is not engaged under this Agreement as a delivery driver, courier, or final-mile carrier, and is not required to operate a vehicle, collect packages from PO Boxes, purchase flowers, or meet customers or gift recipients.

4.2 Contractor agrees to:

(a) Maintain an approved **wrapping location** and agreed **handoff windows** so that the person Wrrapd assigns for drop-off and collection may **bring inbound packages** and later **collect finished wrapped gifts**. Packages are brought to Contractor and collected when wrapping is done — Contractor does **not** pick up from PO Boxes or hand off to common carriers;

(b) When beginning work on a package, inspect for visible damage, shortage, or suspected tampering, and report the same to Wrrapd promptly and in any event within the timeframe published in Platform standards (and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery);

(c) Professionally gift-wrap items to Wrrapd brand standards using approved materials, techniques, and any order-specific designs or messages. If flowers are present on an order card for wrapping together with a gift, Contractor shall wrap or present them as the order instructions require; Contractor does **not** purchase flowers;

(d) Start a **live session** and record and upload clear **video and/or other proof** through the WrapStar App (or other Platform channel Wrrapd designates) as required by Section 6;

(e) Affix the Platform **barcode / delivery label** Wrrapd provides (or that the WrapStar App generates) to each finished gift, include the label in finished-wrap documentation, and stage wrapped orders for collection by the person Wrrapd assigns, according to Platform / WrapStar App instructions. Contractor is **not** required to transport goods to a recipient’s address and shall **not** contact customers or giftees;

(f) Maintain a clean, professional wrapping workspace; and

(g) Install and use the WrapStar App on a compatible smartphone, keep the App reasonably up to date, and follow in-App workflows for availability, handoff windows, status updates, communications, live session, and proof uploads.

4.3 Wrrapd may update published wrapping standards and workflows prospectively via the Platform or email. Any future expansion of Contractor’s role to include delivery would require a separate written agreement (or amendment) expressly covering driving and delivery.

## 5A. Refusal, Detention, and Demand-for-Return

5A.1 **Duty to perform or release.** If Contractor will not wrap an accepted order, or will not complete wrapping already begun, Contractor shall say so in the WrapStar App **immediately** and shall make every item in Contractor’s possession available for pickup by the person Wrrapd assigns within **two (2) hours** of that notice (or the shorter window then published in the App). Silence, delay, or “I will wrap it later” after a refusal is not compliance.

5A.2 **No lien.** Contractor has **no right** to hold, detain, hide, or condition the return of goods for unpaid compensation, pace disputes, ratings, or any other claim against Wrrapd. Pay disputes are resolved under this Agreement and the Mutual Arbitration Agreement — not by keeping merchandise.

5A.3 **Demand.** Wrrapd may demand return of any goods at any time, with or without terminating this Agreement. Contractor shall stage the goods at the door or wrapping entrance of the approved location for the person Wrrapd sends. Failure to make the goods available by the stated deadline is **conversion** and unauthorized detention. Wrrapd may: (a) report the matter to law enforcement; (b) withhold, offset, or claw back compensation; (c) charge Contractor the replacement cost of the goods plus wrap, materials, and makeup delivery; and (d) terminate this Agreement immediately.

5A.4 **Access.** Contractor has no right to require Wrrapd personnel or any person Wrrapd assigns to enter Contractor’s home. Goods must be staged at the door or wrapping entrance.

5A.5 This Section 5A is a material term. Breach is grounds for immediate suspension and termination, in addition to any civil or criminal remedies.

## 6. Live Session, Recording, and Proof (Non-Negotiable)

6.1 **Live session.** For every order, Contractor shall start a **live session** in the WrapStar App **before unboxing** and keep the session running through inspection, wrapping, sealing, barcode affix, and the finished-gift photo. Wrrapd may observe the session in real time. A recording is stored. The camera shall stay on the gift and wrapping surface, not on housemates, children, or private areas of the home. If the live link drops, Contractor shall **stop wrapping**, restart the session, and then continue.

6.2 For every order, Contractor shall also create and submit clear proof **through the WrapStar App** (or another Platform channel Wrrapd designates) in the format(s) required by the Platform, which may include video, photographs, or both, showing:

(a) **Unboxing / inspection** — opening of the retailer package (or beginning work on the item) and the condition of contents;  
(b) **Wrapping process** — wrapping consistent with brand standards; and  
(c) **Finished gift** — completed exterior wrap (and flowers or add-ons if applicable), including the affixed barcode / delivery label.

6.3 Contractor is **not** required to video-record or photograph exchanges with the person Wrrapd assigns for drop-off or collection. Chain-of-custody at those transfer points is handled through Platform / WrapStar App status updates.

6.4 Failure to start or maintain the live session, or to provide complete, timely, and authentic proof under Section 6.2, may result in non-payment for the affected order, chargeback or clawback of amounts already paid, immediate suspension, and/or termination.

6.5 Contractor grants Wrrapd a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, store, reproduce, and display live-session media and proof media for quality assurance, customer support, dispute resolution, claims handling, training, marketing (in anonymized or consented form where required), legal compliance, and operation of the Platform. Contractor shall not delete required proof from Platform systems. Live sessions are for Wrrapd staff only and are not a public stream.

## 8. Compensation

8.1 Contractor is paid an **hourly rate**, not a per-order piece rate. The rate is determined by the **Compensation Schedule** then published in the WrapStar App or on the Platform (including ZIP-specific rates: exact ZIP, then 3-digit prefix, then the WrapStar default). Wrrapd may change published rates prospectively.

8.2 **Minimum paid hour.** For each accepted wrap window in which Contractor finishes at least one gift (or otherwise performs assigned wrapping work as the WrapStar App records), Contractor is paid **not less than one (1) hour** at the applicable hourly rate, even if fewer than twelve finished gifts are assigned or completed in that window (for example, four gifts still earn one full paid hour). Wrrapd does not guarantee volume or income beyond this floor for accepted windows actually worked.

8.3 **Pace and fractional hours.** The wrapping conversion rate is **twelve (12) finished gifts = one (1) paid hour**. Paid wrapping hours for a window equal the **greater of** (a) one (1) hour, or (b) finished gifts ÷ 12, including fractional hours. **Example:** thirty-seven (37) finished gifts earn **three (3) hours plus one-twelfth (1/12) of an hour**. A gift is finished only when it is wrapped to published standards, documented under Section 6, labeled (including the delivery barcode), and ready for collection.

8.4 **Incentive compensation.** From time to time — particularly during higher-demand periods — Wrrapd may offer **discretionary incentive or bonus compensation**, including additional pay for wrapping above the twelve-gifts-per-hour pace, peak-period bonuses, or other incentives published in the Compensation Schedule, Command Center, or WrapStar App. Incentive terms, amounts, eligibility, and duration are set solely by Wrrapd, apply only for the periods stated, and create no ongoing entitlement after an incentive period ends.

8.5 **Materials.** Ordinary wrapping supplies — including gift-wrapping paper, small boxes when needed, scissors, cutters, and tape — are Contractor’s responsibility unless Wrrapd expressly provides a material or reimbursement in writing for a given order.

8.6 Wrrapd may withhold, offset, or claw back compensation for fraud, failed or fabricated proof, skipped live session, chargebacks, amounts attributable to Contractor’s negligence or willful misconduct under Section 5 or 5A, overpayments, no-show costs under Section 16A, or other amounts Contractor owes Wrrapd.

8.7 Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes on compensation. Contractor shall complete Form W-9 (or equivalent) before payouts.

8.8 Tips are not part of WrapStar compensation unless the Compensation Schedule later states otherwise.

## 16A. Weekly Availability; Unavailability; Cancellation; Return of Goods

16A.5 Illness, bereavement, or emergency does **not** excuse the notice or return duties in this Section 16A. Wrrapd’s use of backup contractors is an operations matter and does not create a right to keep goods or to be paid for a missed window.
