# Sick / no-show coverage (Command Center)

Internal ops playbook. **Not** in applicant PDFs.

## Contractor duties (legal)

- **Weekly availability:** submit next week (Mon–Sun) in the app by **Friday 6:00 p.m. Eastern**. Missed deadline → treated unavailable for allocation (admin can force dates on WrapStars).
- **Immediate notice** for illness, bereavement, or emergency — cancel as soon as they know, and **no later than 4 hours before** window start.
- **Return all deliverable materials** within **2 hours** for backup pickup (door / wrapping entrance). Detention = personal liability + clawback + possible termination.

Late or silent no-show: that window unpaid; Wrrapd may recover reasonable last-minute reassignment cost; repeated no-shows = termination.

---

## Command Center

1. **Availability** (`/admin/availability`) — click a day to see WrapStar / JoyRider morning & afternoon staffing and who missed the Friday deadline.
2. Coverage for that **ZIP** / metro.
3. Reassign the window to another **active** contractor who submitted availability (or admin-forced).
4. If none:
   - Delay the shopper promise, **or**
   - Pull overflow from the same metro (wrap + logistics across two people, or a WrapRider).
5. Shopper copy stays short: **“We’re adjusting your delivery window.”** No contractor names. No illness story. No internal systems.

## Goods already in someone’s care

If the contractor already has packages:

- Demand return under the agreement (§5A / §16A): stage at the door / wrapping entrance / curb within **2 hours**.
- Backup picks up. If they refuse or go silent: treat as detention — withhold/claw pay, charge replacement + makeup delivery, consider police/civil path. Use live-session / scan / photo proof you already have.

## Same-day illness after the 4-hour cutoff

Still unpaid for that window. Still try to reassign. Do not invent a shopper excuse beyond the adjusting-window line.

## Tracking the pattern

Note the cancel/no-show on the roster row. A second silent no-show in a short window is grounds to deactivate pending review.
