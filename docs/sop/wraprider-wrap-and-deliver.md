# WrapRider SOP — live wrap and delivery

Internal. Shopper pages never mention live video, barcodes, or contractor names.

A WrapRider does **both** halves in the WrapRider App.

---

## Wrap half

Follow the same live-wrap sequence as wrapping-only work, in the **WrapRider App**:

1. Start live **before unboxing**.
2. Inspect → wrap → seal.
3. Affix the barcode on the side. Order ref only on the wrap — no address.
4. Finished photo including the label.
5. End live. If the live link drops, stop wrapping and restart.

When you are also the courier hop, you may drop inbound packages at your own wrapping location and later collect finished wraps from that location.

If you will not wrap: say so in the App immediately and stage every item at the door or wrapping entrance within **2 hours**.

## Delivery half

Same scan and door sequence as a logistics window, without a second app:

1. Completeness scan of **every** expected code before leaving for the giftee.
2. Flowers: Yes / No / pick up first / already combined.
3. Door scan + photo.
4. Do not enter the home.

If you accept a load and then will not deliver: say so immediately and stage the goods.

## Cancel / sick

Cancel an accepted wrap **or** delivery window as soon as you know, and no later than **4 hours** before start. See [sick-coverage.md](./sick-coverage.md).

## Hours

One WrapRider hourly rate. 12-gifts/hour pace on **wrapping hours only**. Delivery hours have no pace.
