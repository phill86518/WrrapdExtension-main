# WrapStar SOP — live wrap, barcode, collection

Internal. Shopper pages never mention live video, barcodes, or contractor names.

---

## Before the window

1. Confirm the accepted wrap window in the WrapStar App.
2. If you cannot work: cancel in the App as soon as you know, and **no later than 4 hours before start**. See [sick-coverage.md](./sick-coverage.md).
3. Stage a clean wrapping surface. Charge the phone. Test camera and internet.

## During the window

1. **Start live** in the WrapStar App **before unboxing**. Wrrapd may watch in real time. A recording is stored.
2. Camera stays on the gift and wrapping surface — not housemates, children, or private rooms.
3. Inspect. Report damage, shortage, or tampering in the App before wrapping.
4. Wrap to the order card.
5. Seal. **Affix the barcode** on the side of the wrap. Human-readable line is the order ref only — no address on the wrap.
6. Include the label in the finished-gift photo.
7. **End live** only after the photo is uploaded and the App marks the unit labeled.
8. If the live link drops: **stop wrapping**, restart the session, then continue.

An order is not ready for collection until every wrap unit is labeled.

## If you will not wrap

Say so in the App immediately. Stage every item at the door or wrapping entrance within **2 hours**. Do not keep goods for a pay dispute. Do not require anyone to enter your home.

## Collection

The person Wrrapd assigns collects finished wraps in the published handoff window. You do not video the handoff. You do not contact the giftee.

## After the window

Hours are the accepted wrap window, prorated to 15 minutes, with the 12-gifts/hour pace. Until the hours ledger ships, Command Center rates still apply and ops can batch by hand.
