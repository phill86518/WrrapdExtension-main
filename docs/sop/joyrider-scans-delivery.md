# JoyRider SOP — pickup, completeness scan, door

Internal. Shopper pages never mention barcodes, PO Boxes, or contractor names.

---

## Before the window

1. Confirm the accepted logistics window in the JoyRider App.
2. If you cannot work: cancel in the App as soon as you know, and **no later than 4 hours before start**. See [sick-coverage.md](./sick-coverage.md).
3. License, registration, and legally required auto insurance must be current.

## Inbound and wrapping-location hops

1. Collect inbound packages from the Wrrapd location the App shows (PO Box, hub, or other address).
2. Drop them at the wrapping location during the agreed window.
3. Later, collect finished wraps. When the App says flowers are needed: pick them up first, or confirm they are already combined.

## Completeness scan (before leaving for the giftee)

1. Open the stop in the JoyRider App.
2. Scan **every** expected code for that stop. Codes are one per wrap unit, plus a flower-only code when there is no wrap (`WR-{orderRef}-{n}` / `WR-{orderRef}-FL` when product ships).
3. The App shows progress (example: `2/3 gifts + flowers`). Do **not** mark en-route until 100%.
4. After a valid scan, the App shows the address and whether flowers go with that gift.
5. Wrong-route scan: **Wrong gift — check the label.** Do not open the wrap.

Until per-item completeness ships, scan the order label you have and still capture door photo proof. See [../JOYRIDER-BARCODE-DELIVERY.md](../JOYRIDER-BARCODE-DELIVERY.md) and [../PRODUCT-LATER-BARCODES-HOURS-LIVEWRAP.md](../PRODUCT-LATER-BARCODES-HOURS-LIVEWRAP.md).

## At the door

1. Scan again at the door as the App requires.
2. Take the delivery photo.
3. Do not enter the home. Do not discuss other orders.

## If you will not deliver

Say so in the App immediately. Stage every item for pickup within **2 hours**. Do not keep goods for a pay dispute.

## Hours

Accepted logistics windows, prorated to 15 minutes. No 12-gift pace on delivery hours.
