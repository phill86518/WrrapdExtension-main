# Facebook ad images

Check the finished JPEG before it is posted. Do not publish from a prompt alone.

## Logo

- Use the role’s own file: `wordpress/icons/WrapStar.png`, `JoyRider.png`, or `WrapRider.png`.
- Place it small in the **top-left** corner. It must not take over the picture.
- Keep the whole mark inside the frame, including both arms of the W. Do not crop it.
- The wordmark in those files is black. On navy (`#0c0638`) it disappears. Recolor that wordmark to cream (`#fff6e2`) before placing it on navy or on a dark plate.

## Type

- Role names are our words. Write them in double quotes: "WrapStar", "JoyRider", "WrapRider".
- Headline and subline are cream or white, with a dark shadow if they sit on a photo.
- Do not set black or navy type on the navy background.
- No city names, no state names, and no dollar amounts. Pay is hourly, and the rate is confirmed at approval.

## Picture

- One different person for each role. Do not reuse the same face.
- "WrapStar" is wrapping. "JoyRider" is delivering a finished gift. "WrapRider" wraps and carries the gift to the door.
- **Apply now** is the link under the picture. The gold button is the label.

## Before posting

Open the saved JPEG and confirm all of the following:

1. The logo is small and in the top-left.
2. Both arms of the W are visible.
3. The role name on the logo and in the headline can be read.
4. The three ads do not show the same person.
